# Code of Conduct

OpenFolks follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

In this workspace:

- Critique code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks — in issues, PRs, commits, or chat.
- Assume good faith, especially with first-time contributors. Maintainers have finite time.

Report privately to **soni.mil2001@gmail.com**. Reports stay confidential. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that break this code, and may ban repeat offenders.
