# Code of Conduct

Everyone in OpenFolks spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and related chat — is expected to treat others with respect.

## Standards

- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Assume good faith, especially with first-time contributors.
- Do not share others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who repeatedly violates it.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately by contacting the maintainer on GitHub:

https://github.com/manasdutta04

Reports stay confidential. We will review them and take action we believe is appropriate.
