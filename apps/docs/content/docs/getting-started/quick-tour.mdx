## 1. Sidebar

Folks, rooms, automations, connections, and settings. Search covers names and message history. **New** creates a folk or opens a shared room.

## 2. Conversation

The work log. Replies, questions, approvals, tool progress, attachments, and failures stay on the thread. Pin, copy, reply, search, and retry from the message.

## 3. Tasks

**Task** starts a separate unit of work without losing the main thread. A task keeps its own status and result — useful for parallel or handed-off work.

## 4. Model picker

Each folk picks its own engine and model. Cloud CLIs and local engines sit in separate groups. The picker shows what this machine actually has installed.

<ProductScreenshot name="docs-model-picker" alt="OpenFolks model picker showing cloud and local engines" caption="Models are per folk. A crew can mix engines without a global switch." />

## 5. Computer and Backstage

Open the folk's **computer** to choose where visual work runs. Open **Backstage** to watch the live turn tape and engine protocol.

## 6. Connections and routines

Connected apps become tools. Routines turn a good brief into weekday or webhook work.
